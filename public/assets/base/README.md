# Pebble printable base (authored)

Dead-simple differential-drive chassis for the home chore bot.
Units: **millimeters**. Match `src/robot/dims.ts`.

## Design
- Octagonal plates, flat-to-flat **280 mm**, thickness **3.5 mm**
- Standoffs **55 mm** tall → overall stack **62 mm**
- Side motor pods for cheap geared DC motors
- Rear caster mount pocket
- Mast **180 mm** + camera shelf
- SO-101 mount pad on front-right of top plate (mount_x=-90, mount_z=40)

## Bought (not printed)
- Rubber tires / complete wheels (~70 mm OD) — hubs are printed, tires are purchased
- Geared DC motors (diff-drive pair)
- Swivel caster (~28 mm)
- CSI/USB camera module
- Fasteners (M3), Pi, battery, motor driver

## Filament estimate (PLA, ~20% infill)
Rough: plates + pods + mast + hubs ≈ **350–450 g**. Order a 1 kg spool with margin.

## Sim mapping
Same STLs are copied to `public/assets/base/` and loaded by `src/components/ImportedRobots.tsx`
via Three.js `STLLoader` at scale **0.001** (mm→m), posed per `dims.ts`.

Generated by `/tmp/pebble-print/gen_base_stls.py` (numpy-stl primitives).
