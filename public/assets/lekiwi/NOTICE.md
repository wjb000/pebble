# LeKiwi — real printable omni base (Apache-2.0)

**Upstream:** [SIGRobotics-UIUC/LeKiwi](https://github.com/SIGRobotics-UIUC/LeKiwi)

- Sim twin loads the **CAD URDF** (`LeKiwi.urdf` + `meshes/*.stl`) — these are the Onshape-exported parts, not box envelopes.
- Print these STLs from `print/lekiwi/` (same repo `3DPrintMeshes/`).
- Omni wheels in the URDF are the purchased 4″ omni CAD bodies (buy those; print hubs/mounts).
